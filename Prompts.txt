PROJECT SUMMARY
On weekends customers wait 1-2 hours in a salon without knowing their turn.
The app lets customers join a queue from their phone, see live position and
estimated wait, and get called. Reception and barbers manage the queue, and a
waiting-area TV shows who is being served.

USERS: Customer, Receptionist/Owner, Barber.

STACK
React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS (frontend), Node + Express backend,
SQLite for storage, Socket.IO for real-time sync. One command
(`npm run dev`) must start frontend and backend together. Frontend is
deployable on Vercel and backend on Render/Railway later (no localhost URLs
hard-coded; use environment variables).

BUSINESS RULES (enforced on the BACKEND, in ONE shared module)
- Statuses: Waiting -> Called -> In chair -> Done (never backwards);
  Waiting -> Cancelled; Called -> No-show. Reject invalid transitions with a
  clear error.
- First come, first served. "Call next" for barber X picks the earliest
  Waiting customer who chose X or "Any". A customer who chose Ravi is never
  given to another barber.
- A barber has only one customer in the chair at a time.
- Estimated wait = total minutes of services ahead / number of barbers
  working (not on break/off duty).
- One active spot per phone number. A duplicate join returns the existing
  spot, never a new one.
- Called but absent for 10 minutes -> receptionist can mark No-show. If the
  customer arrives later they are re-added at the END with a new token.
- Tokens are S-1, S-2... sequential and reset daily (Asia/Kolkata timezone).
- Online joining closes 30 minutes before closing time (default 9 PM);
  walk-ins are still allowed. If the owner closes the queue, the Join button is
  disabled with a clear message.
- Barber on break: customers who chose that barber see "<Name> is on break".
- Totals, wait times and tokens are computed on the backend, never trusted
  from the client.

DON'T BUILD
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp, loyalty program,
multiple branches.
